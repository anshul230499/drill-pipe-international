API Drill Pipe

New and used API 5DP drill pipe in a wide range of sizes, weights, and grades. Suitable for vertical, directional, and horizontal drilling applications. Range 2 and Range 3 lengths available.

  • Sizes: 2⅜" to 6⅝" OD
  • Grades: E-75, X-95, G-105, S-135
  • Tool Joints: NC, FH, IF connections
  • Range 2 (27–30 ft) & Range 3 (38–45 ft)
  • New, Premium, & Used conditions

Heavy Weight Drill Pipe (HWDP)

Transition pipe between drill collars and standard drill pipe. Heavier wall and full-length wear pads reduce bending stress in the drill string. Particularly important in directional and extended-reach wells.

  • Sizes: 3½" to 5½"
  • Center wear pad — reduces buckling
  • Reduces fatigue failures in transitions
  • Full-length upsets for tool joint strength
  • Grades: Grades per specification

IN-HOUSE THROUGH OUR GROUP COMPANIES

DPI is backed by the manufacturing network of Jindal SAW. Piercing, upsetting, heat treatment, tool-joint forging, precision machining, friction welding and inspection are performed within Jindal group operations — not outsourced as a supply-chain model.

01 / HOT FINISHING
01

Seamless Piercing

Steel billets are heated, pierced and rolled into seamless hollows. Jindal SAW's Nashik facility lists a conical piercing mill and hot-finishing equipment.

02 / PIPE ENDS
02

Upsetting

Hot forming strengthens the pipe ends for the tool-joint interface. Internal, external and internal-external upset configurations can be selected for the required product.

03 / MATERIAL PROPERTIES
03

Heat Treatment

Controlled quenching and tempering support the required yield strength and mechanical properties, with verification through inspection and testing.

04 / CONNECTION COMPONENTS
04

Tool-Joint Forging

Tool joints are produced and finished within the Jindal group manufacturing chain, then precision-machined for the specified pin and box connections.

05 / ASSEMBLY & QUALITY
05

Friction Welding & Inspection

Automated friction welding joins the tool joints to the pipe body. Weld-zone heat treatment, CNC preparation and nondestructive inspection complete the production cycle.

06 / IN THE FIELD
06

Ready for the Rig

Finished API drill pipe and heavy weight drill pipe support drilling programs from conventional wells to demanding directional and extended-reach applications.

Who We Are

Integrated Manufacturing, Built for Drilling

Drill Pipe International, LLC is a Baytown, Texas-based drill pipe manufacturer and a wholly owned subsidiary of Jindal SAW USA, LLC, within the Jindal SAW group. We draw upon the group’s integrated steel tubular manufacturing capabilities for in-house production of drill pipe and heavy weight drill pipe.

DPI operates from Baytown, Texas, serving U.S. and international drilling programs. We focus on drill pipe and heavy weight drill pipe, with available grades, connection options, inspection documentation and supply terms matched to customer requirements.
