01

Seamless Piercing

Billets are heated and pierced to form seamless hollow tubes using Jindal’s conical piercing mill.

02

Upsetting

Pipe ends are hot-formed to provide the required wall thickness and connection geometry.

03

Heat Treatment

Quenching and tempering develop the mechanical properties required for demanding drilling service.

04

Tool-Joint Forging

Connections begin with forged steel components, followed by precision machining.

05

Welding & Inspection

Friction-welded tool joints, weld-zone treatment, CNC finishing and nondestructive inspection.

06

Ready for the Rig

Finished drill pipe is prepared for customer specifications and field operations.
