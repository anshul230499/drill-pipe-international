# Manufacturing research and creative direction — DPI (November 2026)

## Relationship and verified group operations

- Jindal SAW's March 2026 DPI financial statements identify **Drill Pipe International, LLC** as a **wholly owned subsidiary of Jindal SAW USA, LLC**, ultimately indirectly owned by Jindal SAW Ltd., India. https://jindalsaw.com/docs/Drill-Pipe-International-LLC_March-31-2026.pdf
- Jindal SAW seamless operations at **Nashik, India** list rotary-hearth furnace, **conical piercing mill**, finishing and stretch reducing, plus an **upsetter**, quench-and-temper line, and machining. https://jindalsaw.com/business-operations/products/pipes-tubes-carbon-steel-alloy-steel-seamless/
- Jindal SAW USA documents external/internal/IEU upsetting, full-length quench-and-temper treatment, 100% NDE on pipe bodies, MPI on upset ends, CNC end preparation, **automated friction welding** of tool joints, weld zone heat treatment, with optional hardbanding and internal coatings. https://www.jindalsawusa.net/ and https://jindalsawusa.net/services/drill-pipe
- Jindal SAW lists Drill Pipe International at Baytown, manufacturing API drill pipe from 2-3/8 in to 6-5/8 in, and heavy-weight drill pipe up to 5-1/2 in. https://jindalsaw.com/business-operations/key-subsidiaries-joint-ventures/jindal-pipe-usa-inc/
- The company's owner confirms that piercing, upsetting, heat treatment, forging, assembly, etc. are **in-house across Jindal group companies**, rather than outsourced. This internal confirmation is the basis of the broader integrated supply-chain claim; published pages independently document many constituent processes but not every facility or production route.

## Peer benchmarking and insights (NOT website claims about DPI)

1. **Grant Prideco / NOV**: Technical depth, fully integrated sourcing of green tube and tool joints, product configurations, traceability, clear connection specifications. https://www.nov.com/our-business-units/grant-prideco and https://www.nov.com/products/gp-hdd-drill-pipe
2. **Texas Steel Conversion**: Process pages for forging/upsetting, quench and temper, CNC finishing, on-site photos, direct emphasis on what the manufacturing equipment actually does. https://texassteelconversion.com/forging/ and https://texassteelconversion.com/texas-steel-conversion-company/
3. **Command Tubular**: Strong rig photography, concise product families, easy navigation, and disciplined technical product content. https://commandtubular.com/ and https://commandtubular.com/products/product-lines/drill-pipes/
4. **Hilong**: Specific inspection language (100% testing of pipe and weld zones), integration of tool joints, coatings, hardbanding, upsetting, heat treatment and welding. https://www.hilonggroup.com/en/product/info/chgzg
5. **DP-Master**: Product-focused technical explanations of upset geometry, coating, hardbanding, thread finishing and connection testing. Its drill-pipe manufacturing plant is identified in Jiangyin, China. https://www.dpmaster.com.sg/products_uri/drill-pipe/ and https://www.dpmaster.com.sg/about-us/corporate-overview/
6. **Nabors and Precision Drilling**: Rig-side visuals and real-world reliability/performance storytelling. Avoid implying an existing customer relationship. https://www.nabors.com/our-company/what-we-do/ and https://www.precisiondrilling.com/
